HL ART GALLERY

Privacy information

How this gallery currently handles information.

Artist submissions and profiles

The submission form collects the information you provide, including creative and full names, email, location, optional contact and portfolio details, an artist statement, artwork information, the uploaded image and your declarations. Submissions and review notes are stored for gallery administration. They are not automatically published.

Approved public artwork can include the title, artist name, collection, image, description, medium, year, approved biography and publication dates. Private email, full application details and internal review notes are not included in the public gallery response.

Browser preferences and cookies

Save and Follow preferences are stored in this browser’s local storage. Gallery votes, competition choices, appreciations and report limits use a randomly generated guest cookie and a server-side hash of that browser identifier. These controls do not establish a verified personal identity, and clearing cookies or using another browser can bypass browser-based limits.

Artist studio sign-in uses an emailed, short-lived link and a secure session cookie. The private admin token stays in the open admin page and is not intentionally saved to browser storage.

Reports and moderation

A report records its artwork, reason, description, browser identifier hash and time. Its contents, review status and administrator notes are private to authorized administrators. Public pages do not display reporters, report details or moderation notes. Please avoid including sensitive personal information in a report.

Service providers and sharing

Netlify hosts the site, functions and uploaded content. Neon provides the gallery interaction database. Artist sign-in emails use Resend when configured. These providers process the information needed to deliver their services and may maintain operational logs.

Share buttons open your device’s sharing options or copy a public link. A room photo selected in the room planner is handled in that browser and is not uploaded by the planner.

Retention and requests

The gallery currently has no automatic deletion schedule for submissions, interaction records or moderation reports. Records remain until reviewed for removal by the owner. You can contact the gallery about access, correction or removal; a request may require identity or ownership verification.

Removing a published image cannot guarantee removal of copies someone previously downloaded or shared. Clearing browser data removes local Save/Follow preferences and sessions, but does not delete gallery server records.

Payments and updates

Production print purchases and automatic fulfillment are not open. Do not enter real payment details into sandbox checkout. This information describes the current build and should be reviewed whenever the site’s services or data handling change.
