Submission information

What happens when you share artwork with HL Art Gallery.

You keep ownership

Submitting artwork does not transfer copyright or ownership to HL Art Gallery. You must have created the work or have permission from its rights holder to submit it for review. Any future sale, licensing, commission or collaboration requires separate terms agreed with the artist.

Prepare your submission

Upload one JPEG, PNG or WebP image up to 4 MB. Give the artwork a title, choose a medium and share an artist statement that describes the creative work. Use accurate information and describe AI-assisted work honestly. Do not include private records, other people’s personal details or unrelated sensitive information.

Review and publication

Submissions are reviewed privately for rights declarations and content concerns. Automated checks are preliminary; an administrator makes the publication decision. Missing rights declarations or unresolved moderation flags block publication. Approval is not guaranteed and is not a promise of representation, sales or a competition place.

Approved public information can appear in the feed, gallery and artist profile. You can use Artist studio to request a biography update for review. Administrators can hold or unpublish work when concerns need review.

Community participation

Appreciations, saved artwork and gallery votes do not automatically enter an artwork in a competition. Competition entries are selected separately and follow that competition’s closing time and displayed voting rules.

Prints and editions

A published image is not automatically a sale-ready print file. Large prints need a suitable original file, confirmed format, supplier specifications, pricing and fulfillment checks. Gallery display frames do not promise a physical frame. Print sales and automatic fulfillment are not open in production.

Changes or concerns

Contact the gallery with your artist reference for a correction or removal request. Read the privacy information to understand what is public and what remains private.
