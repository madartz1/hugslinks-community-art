Prompt set:
- Ascension: a single black swan rising from dark water, two wings lifted into an expansive V, graceful curved neck, antique gold edges and gold reflections; matte obsidian impasto and palette-knife metallic accents; portrait 4:5; edge-to-edge artwork, no text, frame, room, signature or watermark.
- Stillwater: one black swan gliding right on an obsidian lake, head lowered toward its gold-flecked reflection, lower-third composition with quiet black negative space; matte oil texture and restrained gold-leaf light; portrait 4:5; no text, frame, room, signature or watermark.
- Together in Gold: two black swans facing inward with curved necks making an open heart-shaped space, two separate bodies and two heads; antique gold feather accents and shared gold ripples; matte textured oil; portrait 4:5; no text, frame, room, signature or watermark.

The original generated PNGs are retained separately; these JPEGs are web display assets. Pixel dimensions alone do not establish suitability for a large canvas print. Titles and descriptive copy are proposals for artist approval.
