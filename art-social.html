BEHIND THE FRAME

Meet Swan the Artist

Grace. Resilience. A little light in the darkness.

Rooted in Bronx creativity and the spirit of community, Swan explores identity, transformation and human connection. Black Gold brings those themes into focus through expressive swans, deep shadows and traces of gold.

Each work offers a different invitation: rise with Ascension, pause with Stillwater, or find connection in Together in Gold.
