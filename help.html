HL ART GALLERY

Gallery help

Find your way around HL Art Gallery.

Explore the artwork

The feed introduces featured and newly published work. Gallery lets you browse published pieces and their gallery vote totals. Collections brings artworks together with room displays and exhibition views.

Tap a framed piece or View artwork to open a larger image and read its description. If 3D viewing is unavailable, use the artwork catalogue and room displays.

Appreciate, Save and Vote

Appreciate ♥
A positive reaction, separate from gallery and competition votes. Tap again to remove it. Reactions use a browser cookie to limit duplicates; they do not verify one person across devices.
Save
A personal collection stored in this browser on this device. It does not transfer to another phone or browser.
Follow
Shows an artist’s work in your Following filter on this device. It does not create a public follower account.
Vote
Gallery votes and competition choices are separate. Each browser can vote once per gallery artwork and once in each competition. A competition choice cannot be changed.

Report a concern

Use Report beneath an artwork to describe a copyright, harmful-content, privacy or other concern. Reports go to a private administrator queue and do not automatically hide artwork. You may submit one report per artwork per browser per day. Contact the gallery if the form is unavailable or more information is needed.

For artists

Submit an image and artist introduction for private review. Use Artist studio with the same submission email to request a sign-in link and view your submission status. Publication requires admin approval.

See submission information before uploading.

Print editions

Print pages currently show proposed editions. Production checkout and automatic print fulfillment are not open. Digital frames do not indicate an included physical frame. Any sandbox payment test uses separate preview checkout and does not produce a print.
