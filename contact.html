Contact the gallery

Questions, artwork concerns and support.

Email HL Art Gallery

info@hugslinks.com

Tell us which artwork or page you are asking about and what you need help with. Include your artist reference if the question concerns a submission. Do not send passwords, admin tokens, card details or sensitive personal records.

This opens your email app. The gallery does not promise an immediate response or provide an emergency service.

Artwork and copyright concerns

The Report control under a published artwork sends a private concern to administrators. For a detailed copyright or privacy request, email the artwork title, its page link, the concern and a way to reach you. Avoid posting private evidence in public descriptions.

Submission or account help

Use the email address from your submission when requesting artist studio access. If a link does not arrive, check spam and contact us with your submission reference. Do not forward private sign-in links.

Privacy requests

You can request a correction, removal or information about a submission or report by email. The gallery may need to verify your connection to the record before acting. Clearing browser storage removes this device’s saved and followed preferences; it does not remove server records.
